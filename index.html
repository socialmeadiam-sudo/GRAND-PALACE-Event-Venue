Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding event planning at Grand Palace.

What type of events can I inquire about at Grand Palace?
You can inquire about a wide range of functions including Wedding Receptions, Mehndi nights, Nikah ceremonies, Engagement functions, Birthdays, and Corporate banquets.
Where is Grand Palace located?
Grand Palace is conveniently located at 42-1/C, Main Shahra-e-Faisal, Block 6 P.E.C.H.S., Karachi, Pakistan.
Can I inquire about a wedding event?
Yes, wedding receptions are one of our main inquiry categories. You can share your expected guest numbers and preferred date to get started.
Can I ask about Mehndi or engagement arrangements?
Absolutely. We welcome inquiries for Mehndi celebrations, colorful stage setups, and refined engagement arrangements.
Can I send my event details through WhatsApp?
Yes, you can use our interactive WhatsApp inquiry builders on this website or directly message us at 03242886757.
Can I provide my preferred event date?
Yes, when submitting your inquiry, you can specify your exact preferred date or date range to check hall availability.
Does sending an inquiry confirm a booking?
No. Sending an inquiry allows us to review your event details and check calendar availability. A booking is only finalized after direct confirmation and formal agreement with Grand Palace.
